---
name: Territorial layers
description: Durable boundaries for expanding the OpenKingdoms map model.
---

OpenKingdoms should treat a county/province as the persistent territorial unit, with settlement, barracks, stronghold, routes, and ownership modeled as distinct layers. Future bandits, civilizations, nation-states, colonies, and growing entities should extend that model rather than replace it.

**Why:** The campaign needs to support peaceful development and multiple political types without making every map feature a single interchangeable “base.”

**How to apply:** When adding a new territorial feature, preserve county selection/index synchronization, give older saves a neutral migration default, and keep strongholds separate from settlement upgrades, barracks, fronts, and diplomacy.
