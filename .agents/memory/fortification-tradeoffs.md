---
name: Fortification tradeoffs
description: Durable design guidance for stronghold progression in the OpenKingdoms campaign.
---

Stronghold progression should make defense compete with the treasury and deployable field capacity: higher tiers can demand recurring upkeep and a permanent garrison rather than being strictly better in every situation.

**Why:** A Citadel is strategically interesting when it protects a valuable county but delays expansion or weakens a field order; upkeep and garrison obligations make that choice legible without forcing players into combat.

**How to apply:** Show construction costs and ongoing obligations before every upgrade, apply upkeep during ordinary turn resolution, reserve garrisoned forces from front staging, and keep open counties fully playable for peaceful campaigns and migrated saves.
